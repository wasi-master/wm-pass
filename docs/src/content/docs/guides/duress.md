title: Duress, decoys and hidden vaults
description: What a duress password, a decoy and a hidden vault do, and the limits of each against someone determined.
---

These are set up under Settings, Security. At the unlock screen, each of their passwords looks like any other.

**A wipe password** erases the vault on this device and shows "Wrong password", as a mistyped password would. WM Pass also forgets the backup places, accounts and paired devices it knew here. Backups and your other devices keep the vault.

**A lockout password** makes the vault refuse every password for an hour, the right one included. Nothing is lost. **Lock and refuse passwords now**, on a computer, does the same from inside the open vault.

**A decoy vault** is a separate vault with its own password, kept among files that look just like it. Nothing in the files says which one is real or how many there are, and the app never shows which one opened. Fill it with a few believable logins.

**The hidden shelf** keeps chosen entries out of every list, search and autofill until you open it with your password. It closes again when you leave its page or the vault locks.

**Self-destructing entries** remove themselves at a time you set.

Both are in an item's **More** menu on a computer, and under **More options** on the phone.

## What these cannot do

They are meant to get you through a quick look under pressure. They do not defeat forensics. Flash storage does not reliably erase, and a disk image taken earlier, or a sync provider's file history, still holds what it held. Someone with an older copy of your files to compare may notice a decoy. An entry that has self-destructed still exists in any copy made before then.
