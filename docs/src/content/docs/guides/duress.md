---
title: Duress, decoys and hidden vaults
description: What a duress password, a decoy and a hidden vault do, and the limits of each against someone determined.
---

**A duress password** looks like any other at the door. Each one does what you chose: open a decoy vault, wipe this device's copy, or lock hard and alert you.

**A hidden vault** is a file among others that look just like it, opened by its own password. The app never shows which one opened.

**The hidden shelf** keeps chosen entries out of every list, search and autofill until you open it with your password.

**Self-destructing entries** remove themselves at a time you set.

Both are under **More options** on an item's page.

## What these cannot do

They are meant to get you through a quick look under pressure. They do not defeat forensics. Flash storage does not reliably erase, and a disk image taken earlier, or a sync provider's file history, still holds what it held. An entry that has self-destructed still exists in any copy made before then.
