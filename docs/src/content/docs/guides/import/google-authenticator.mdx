<Aside type="tip" title="Which file">
Google Authenticator makes no file. It shows QR codes for the accounts you pick, and WM Pass reads those codes, scanned or from screenshots.
</Aside>

<Tabs syncKey="device">
<TabItem label="Android" icon="mobile-android">

Google calls this a transfer. WM Pass takes the place of the "new device" and scans the QR codes.

<Steps>

1. Open Google Authenticator on the phone that holds the codes.
2. Tap **Menu**, then **Transfer codes**.
3. Tap **Export codes**.
4. Unlock the phone when it asks.
5. Select the accounts you want to move.
6. Tap **Next**. The app shows a QR code; with many accounts it shows several, one after another.
7. Leave that screen open and scan each code with WM Pass. If both apps are on the same phone, take a screenshot of each code instead, where your phone allows it.

</Steps>

Steps from [Get verification codes with Google Authenticator](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/1066447?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&hl=en).

</TabItem>

## Good to know

- The QR codes hold the secret keys for your accounts. Anyone who sees or photographs them can make your codes, so delete any screenshot once the import is done.
- A long list of accounts produces several QR codes; scan every one, or some accounts will be missing.
- If your codes are synced to your Google Account, the export still works; it only reads what the app holds on that phone.

## Then, in WM Pass

In WM Pass on Android, open Settings, Import, and tap **Scan Google Authenticator's code**. Scan each code Google shows, then import them together. A screenshot of each code works too: choose it with **Choose a file**. The [import steps](/guides/import-export/#importing-into-wm-pass) cover the rest.
