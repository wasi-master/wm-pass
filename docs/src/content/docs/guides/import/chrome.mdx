<Aside type="tip" title="Which file">
Download the passwords as a CSV file.
</Aside>

<Tabs syncKey="device">
<TabItem label="Android" icon="mobile-android">
<Steps>

1. Open **Chrome** on your phone.
2. Tap **More** (the three dots), then **Settings**.
3. Tap **Autofill and passwords**, then **Google Password Manager**.
4. At the bottom, tap **Settings**.
5. Tap **Export passwords & passkeys**.
6. Tap **Download file**.
7. If asked, confirm with your screen lock (PIN, pattern or fingerprint).

</Steps>

Steps from [Import or export passwords and passkeys with Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/13068232?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="iPhone" icon="phone">

Chrome on iPhone hands the export to another app, such as Files, instead of saving it straight away.

<Steps>

1. Open **Chrome**.
2. Tap **More**, then **Settings**.
3. Tap **Autofill and passwords**, then **Password Manager**.
4. At the bottom left, tap **Settings**, then **Export data**.
5. Select the passwords you want to export, then tap **Continue**.
6. Choose the destination app or location for the exported file.

</Steps>

Steps from [Import or export passwords and passkeys with Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/13068232?co=GENIE.Platform%3DiOS).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Computer" icon="laptop">
<Steps>

1. Open **Chrome** on your computer.
2. At the top right, select **More**, then **Passwords and autofill**.
3. Select **Google Password Manager**, then **Settings**.
4. Scroll to **Export passwords**.
5. Select **Download file**.

</Steps>

Steps from [Import or export passwords and passkeys with Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/13068232?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Good to know

- A CSV carries passwords only; passkeys are not in it.
- Exporting needs a screen lock set on the Android phone; Chrome asks you to set one up if there is none.
- An enterprise policy can turn export off (Google lists this for iPhone and iPad).

## Then, in WM Pass

Open Settings, Import, and choose the file. The [import steps](/guides/import-export/#importing-into-wm-pass) cover every WM Pass app.

Delete the export once WM Pass has read it.
