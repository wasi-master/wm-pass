<Steps>

1. Open the Keeper web vault or desktop app and unlock your vault.
2. Click your email address at the top right, then **Settings**.

   <Shot src="https://914511346-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-LSGVtOTYUIkVBoYtFvK%2Fuploads%2FprnpCQkjBMbCoWmJa3HN%2FScreenshot%202025-01-19%20at%2012.50.02%E2%80%AFPM.png?alt=media&amp;token=f10b6794-d67a-4891-a2e1-bfc06b6d6dcf" alt="Keeper web vault with the account menu open and Settings highlighted" from="https://docs.keeper.io/en/user-guides/export-and-reports/vault-export" publisher="Keeper" />

3. Select **Export** in the left list of the Settings window.

   <Shot src="https://914511346-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2F-LSGVtOTYUIkVBoYtFvK%2Fuploads%2FX5b5PKYFHDQjI10zhIkw%2FScreenshot%202025-01-19%20at%2012.49.34%E2%80%AFPM.png?alt=media&amp;token=7f4a37d3-a596-4c01-943a-ae5ae3e3ac46" alt="Keeper Settings window on Export, with CSV, JSON, PDF and KDBX options" from="https://docs.keeper.io/en/user-guides/export-and-reports/vault-export" publisher="Keeper" />

4. Under **Export File**, choose **JSON**.
5. Tick **Include records shared with me** if you want those records too.
6. Click **Export** beside **Export File**, not the one under Shared Records Report.

</Steps>

## Good to know

- Keeper's export has no file attachments.
- On a company account, the admin's role policy can turn export off.

## Then, in WM Pass

Open Settings, Import, and choose the file. The [import steps](/guides/import-export/#importing-into-wm-pass) cover every WM Pass app.

Delete the export once WM Pass has read it.

If you exported on a computer and use WM Pass on your phone, import the file in WM Pass on that computer and [sync](/guides/backup-sync/), or copy the file to the phone over a USB cable. Email and cloud drives keep copies of what passes through them.
