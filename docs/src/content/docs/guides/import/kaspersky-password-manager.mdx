<Aside type="tip" title="Which file">
Export a **TXT** file. WM Pass reads the text file Kaspersky writes, with its Websites, Applications and Notes sections.
</Aside>

<Tabs syncKey="device">
<TabItem label="Web" icon="external">

This works in any browser, including the one on your phone. Kaspersky's Android help describes no export from the app, so use My Kaspersky there.

<Steps>

1. Sign in to My Kaspersky and open the **Passwords** tab.
2. Enter your main password and click **Unlock storage**.
3. At the top right, click **More actions**.
4. Click **Export to TXT**. Kaspersky also offers **Export to CSV**; choose the TXT one.
5. Read the warning and click **Continue**. The file goes to your Downloads folder.

</Steps>

Steps from [How to import and export data](https://support.kaspersky.com/my-kaspersky/232804).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows" icon="laptop">
<Steps>

1. Open the main window of Kaspersky Password Manager.
2. In the menu panel, click **Additional**, then **Settings**.
3. Open the **Import/Export** section.
4. Under **Export to text file**, click **Export**.
5. Type a file name, pick a folder, and click **OK**.

</Steps>

Steps from [Import and export data](https://support.kaspersky.com/KPM/Win9.2/en-US/130515.htm).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Mac" icon="apple">
<Steps>

1. Open Kaspersky Password Manager.
2. Choose **File**, then **Export to TXT**.
3. Pick a file name and a place, then click **Save**.
4. When the status window shows how many items were exported, click **OK**.

</Steps>

Steps from [Export data](https://support.kaspersky.com/KPM/Mac10.3/en-US/250621.htm).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Good to know

- Kaspersky does not protect the exported file, so anyone who opens it can read your passwords.
- The Mac export includes both active and inactive entries.

## Then, in WM Pass

Open Settings, Import, and choose the file. The [import steps](/guides/import-export/#importing-into-wm-pass) cover every WM Pass app.

Delete the export once WM Pass has read it.

If you exported on a computer and use WM Pass on your phone, import the file in WM Pass on that computer and [sync](/guides/backup-sync/), or copy the file to the phone over a USB cable. Email and cloud drives keep copies of what passes through them.
