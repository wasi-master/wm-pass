<Aside type="tip" title="Which file">
Choose **1PUX**, which brings each item with its attachments, in a folder named after its 1Password vault. WM Pass also reads the CSV that 1Password 8 writes, but that file keeps only logins: their title, web address, username, password, one-time code, notes, tags and favourite mark. It leaves out custom fields such as security questions and backup codes.
</Aside>

<TabItem label="Mac" icon="apple">
<Steps>

1. Open 1Password on your Mac and unlock it.
2. In the menu bar, choose **File**, then **Export**, then pick the account to export.
3. Enter your account password. If you unlock with SSO, choose **Sign in with** your identity provider instead and sign in.
4. Choose **1PUX** as the format, then click **Export Data**.
5. Pick where to save the file, then click **Open**.

</Steps>

Steps from [How to export your data from the 1Password apps](https://support.1password.com/export/).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows" icon="laptop">
<Steps>

## Good to know

- Passkeys can only be exported from the iOS and Android apps, so a desktop export leaves them out. 1Password advises making new passkeys on each site and saving them somewhere else.
- 1Password 7 (retiring in early 2027) exports differently, through File, Export, All Items from one vault, as 1PIF, CSV or TXT. WM Pass reads none of those, since 1Password 7's CSV has different columns. The steps here are for 1Password 8.
- Archived items are left out of a 1PUX import. From the CSV, which has no archive, they come in with the tag Archived, ready to find and clear out.

## Then, in WM Pass

Open Settings, Import, and choose the file. The [import steps](/guides/import-export/#importing-into-wm-pass) cover every WM Pass app.

Delete the export once WM Pass has read it.

If you exported on a computer and use WM Pass on your phone, import the file in WM Pass on that computer and [sync](/guides/backup-sync/), or copy the file to the phone over a USB cable. Email and cloud drives keep copies of what passes through them.
