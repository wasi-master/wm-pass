Choose **Export accounts to file**, which writes a JSON file. The other choice exports to a QR code.

<Tabs syncKey="device">
<TabItem label="Android" icon="mobile-android">

LastPass has no export guide of its own for this app, so these steps come from other apps' guides. They describe the phone app in general, and your version may word a screen differently.

<Steps>

1. Open LastPass Authenticator.
2. Tap the cog to open the settings.
3. Tap **Transfer accounts**.
4. Tap **Export accounts to file**.
5. Save the file where you can pick it from inside WM Pass.

</Steps>

## Good to know

- Bitwarden's page names the screen **Settings**, then **Transfer accounts**; the Extract OTP page adds the cog and the two export choices, a QR code or a file.
- The file holds every secret key in readable form.

## Then, in WM Pass

Open Settings, Import, and choose the file. The [import steps](/guides/import-export/#importing-into-wm-pass) cover every WM Pass app.

Delete the export once WM Pass has read it.
