<Aside type="tip" title="Which file">
Choose **CSV file** as the format.
</Aside>

<Tabs syncKey="device">
<TabItem label="Android" icon="mobile-android">

<Aside>
RoboForm's Android app can't export a CSV. Its Backup and Restore makes a RoboForm backup, which other apps can't read. Use the browser extension or the desktop app on a computer.
</Aside>

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Browser extension" icon="puzzle">
<Steps>

1. Click the **RoboForm** extension icon in your browser, then the three dots in the upper right, and choose **Settings**.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/37483237999885" alt="RoboForm extension menu with Settings" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

2. Open **Account & Data** in the left column, and under **Export data from RoboForm** click **Export**.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/37241283570445" alt="RoboForm Account &amp; Data page with the Export button" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

3. Enter your master password and click **Continue**.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/14081306446989" alt="RoboForm master password prompt" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

4. Confirm the CSV export by clicking **Export**. The file goes to your Downloads folder.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/14081306539917" alt="RoboForm CSV export confirmation" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

5. Click **Close** on the finished message.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/14081306610573" alt="RoboForm export completed message" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

</Steps>

Steps from [How to export your RoboForm logins into a CSV file](https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows" icon="laptop">

You open the desktop app's options from the browser extension.

<Steps>

1. Click the **RoboForm** extension icon in your browser, then the three dots in the upper right, and choose **Settings**.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/37483237999885" alt="RoboForm extension menu with Settings" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

2. Open the **Advanced** tab, scroll to **Open Options in Installed RoboForm** and click **Open**.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/37293433789709" alt="RoboForm Advanced settings with the Open Options in Installed RoboForm button" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

3. In the desktop app, open **Account & Data** and click **Export**.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/14081282182029" alt="RoboForm desktop Account &amp; Data page" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

4. Enter your master password and click **Log In**.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/14081306701965" alt="RoboForm desktop master password prompt" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

5. Select the folders to export, set the format to **CSV file**, tick the data types you want (Logins, Bookmarks, Safenotes) and pick where to save. Choose the format first, because some formats can't be saved to every folder.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/14081306744205" alt="RoboForm export dialog with folders, format and data types" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

6. Click **Export**, then **Yes** to confirm saving an unencrypted CSV.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/14081306794765" alt="RoboForm confirming an unencrypted CSV export" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

7. Click **Close** when it finishes. **Open containing folder** shows the file.

   <Shot src="https://help.roboform.com/hc/article_attachments/14081306842125" alt="RoboForm export finished window" from="https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file" publisher="RoboForm" />

</Steps>

Steps from [How to export your RoboForm logins into a CSV file](https://help.roboform.com/hc/en-us/articles/230425008-How-to-export-your-RoboForm-logins-into-a-CSV-file).

</TabItem>

## Then, in WM Pass

Open Settings, Import, and choose the file. The [import steps](/guides/import-export/#importing-into-wm-pass) cover every WM Pass app.

Delete the export once WM Pass has read it.

If you exported on a computer and use WM Pass on your phone, import the file in WM Pass on that computer and [sync](/guides/backup-sync/), or copy the file to the phone over a USB cable. Email and cloud drives keep copies of what passes through them.
