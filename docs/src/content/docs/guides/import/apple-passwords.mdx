<TabItem label="iPhone" icon="phone">
<Steps>

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Apps**, then **Safari**.
3. Below **History and Website Data**, tap **Export**.
4. Select **Passwords** and deselect the other choices.

   <Shot src="https://ipcdn-web.apple.com/assets/v2/web/7a2ae944-20ed-4c03-9a30-ad7709b8a749" alt="The Export Browsing Data screen, with Passwords selected for export to another password manager" from="https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/export-passwords-iphf28f2e93e/ios" publisher="Apple Support" />

5. Tap **Save to Downloads**.
6. Tap **Done**.

</Steps>

Steps from [Export passwords to another password manager on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/export-passwords-iphf28f2e93e/ios).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Mac" icon="apple">
<Steps>

1. Open the **Passwords** app.
2. Choose **File**, then **Export All Passwords to File**.
3. Click **Export Passwords**.
4. Pick where to save the file, then click **Save**.

</Steps>

Steps from [Export passwords from your Mac to a file](https://support.apple.com/guide/passwords/export-passwords-mchl35b12625/mac).

## Good to know

- Wi-Fi passwords, passwords shared in a group (unless you created the group) and Sign in with Apple accounts are not exported.
- Apple's Mac page also lets you export a single password with File, then Export Selected Password to File; for a move, use Export All.

## Then, in WM Pass

Open Settings, Import, and choose the file. The [import steps](/guides/import-export/#importing-into-wm-pass) cover every WM Pass app.

Delete the export once WM Pass has read it.

If you exported on a computer and use WM Pass on your phone, import the file in WM Pass on that computer and [sync](/guides/backup-sync/), or copy the file to the phone over a USB cable. Email and cloud drives keep copies of what passes through them.
