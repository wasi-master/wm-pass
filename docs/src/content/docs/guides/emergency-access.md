---
title: Emergency access
description: Split a recovery key between people you trust, so that enough of them together can open your vault.
---

Under Settings, Security, **Emergency kit** splits a recovery key into shares. Choose how many people get one and how many are needed together, such as any three of five, then **Make the shares**. Fewer than the required number learn nothing.

**Save the kit** writes every share to one text file. Print it, give each person their own share, then delete the file. The shares are not shown again, so a kit closed without saving has to be made again.

## Opening the vault with shares

The trustees need enough shares and a copy of the vault file, such as a backup. At the unlock screen, under **I already have a vault** or **Add another vault or account**, they choose **Recover with emergency shares**, pick the copy, paste the shares one on each line, and set a new password for it. The vault then opens on that device under the new password.

## The waiting period is advice

Nothing enforces a wait. With no server involved, trustees who hold enough shares can open the vault at once. Agree a wait with them if you want one, and choose people you would trust with the vault today.

Rekeying the vault stops the shares working, so make a new kit afterwards. A trustee holding an old copy of the file can still read that old copy.
