# Brand marks

`brands.tsv` and `brand-paths/` are generated by `tools/icon/gen_brands.py` from the [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) package, version 16.36.0. Simple Icons releases its files under CC0 1.0. The icons it lists under another licence are left out.

Simple Icons takes a logo out when its owner asks. A few very common sites, listed by the script when it runs, come from versions 13.21.0 and 9.21.0 of the package, where they were released under the same terms.

The logos remain the trademarks of their owners. WM Pass draws them only to show which site an entry is for, and implies no endorsement.
